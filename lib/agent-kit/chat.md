How your Telegram chat stays clean (node step 639). In one turn, from a message of the person or of the node to the end of your
answer, only your LAST reply in a chat stays: each earlier reply of the same turn disappears 30 seconds after a later one is sent. A
reply with files always stays.
- The last reply of a turn is the summary. Everything the person must keep goes into it, as one message: the result, the text they
  asked for, the link, the question you are waiting on.
- A short interim line («ищу…», «запускаю…») is fine: it disappears by itself. Never send «Готово» or any tail after the summary — it
  would push the summary out.
- Content and a question go together in the last reply, or the content goes as a file.
The node's own service messages (the agent is on, already off, a command done) disappear the same way; results, questions still
waiting and troubles stay.
