# The field of people near the architect

**Process: both.** The talk is the working process; whatever is born goes through `create-element` (build).
Node step 649; the owner, 2026-11-05: «один архитектор может поднимать под любого участника сообщества и его собственный элемент.
Пользователь может перейти по ссылке создать агента Telegram и в принципе даже никогда не открывать компьютер» — and «это мой самый
желанный сценарий работы проекта». Step 673: this skill gives the facts of the field; the shape comes from the method of
`shape-the-need`.

## Why it matters
Many people have heard of AI and never let it into their lives: a chat is already hard for them, building an agent is out of reach.
One person who can drive the node is enough; everyone else only talks.

## The facts the forces act on
- **Each person is an owner of their own**: an element's memory, bot and data belong to one person (force 5). The architect sees all of
  them; the person's agent sees only its own element — say so to the person.
- **Each person works on their own Claude subscription** — their work never runs on the architect's; Anthropic's services are for
  people of 18 and older; a minor's work does not run on a Claude subscription, and the lawful way for it is the architect's decision,
  never yours (skill `provider-rules`, «The integrator case»).
- **Registration needs the own domain** — on the temporary address it is closed (`/api/domain/activate`, skill `use-domain`).
- **An account belongs to an app**: the person registers on their element's site and becomes its administrator; other elements stay
  invisible to them (`npm run role` when the role is missing).
- **A bot is the person's door**: they make it in @BotFather on their phone and give its token to the architect; the element's Telegram
  page gives the invite link `t.me/<bot>?start=…` (`npm run telegram`).
- **Selling a service** joins Lagrix only at the person's word (`npm run web4`).

## How the work runs once the shape is chosen — one person at a time
1. **Who and what for**, in the architect's words: the person, what their agent should do for them, in which language.
2. **The rules first**, in two sentences — the subscription and the age, from the facts above.
3. **The own domain first**, when the person must register.
4. **Whatever is new is born** — `npm run find`, then `npm run create` (skill `create-element`) with the person's role and job in the
   brief.
5. **The account, then the bot** — as in the facts above.
6. **The work runs**: the element's agent serves its person.

## Laws
- Personal data of the people never goes into git; an order leaves a summary without it (`order:save`).
- The element boundary holds.
- Nothing is offered as working that the node cannot do today: a capability missing — `npm run need` and the skill `handle-need`.
