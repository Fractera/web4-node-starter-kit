# The field of research

**Process: both.** The talk is the working process; whatever is born goes through `create-element` or the hand's own agent (build).
Core step 701; the owner, 2026-11-08: «любое исследование должно быть подкреплено реальными тестами, и осмысленными анализами … сложные
продукты могут проходить стадию исследования в течение продолжительного времени и поэтому сохранять контекст для таких задач становится
важной задачей … И тем более если понадобится перекрёстный анализ». Step 673: this skill gives the facts of the field; which shape the
work takes, the method of `shape-the-need` decides on the node's facts.

## Why it matters
A research told from documentation is a retelling. A research proven on this computer — the SDK installed, a server and a client
started, the API called with a test key — is evidence, and evidence that is kept is what the next research and the next comparison
stand on.

## What the words can mean
- The words «this project» have three readings: an outside product with a link or a name — a research; this node or Fractera — `npm
  run contacts` and `npm run card`, answered from them (CLAUDE.md §4, «Know yourself»); an element of the node — its card and a question
  to its agent (`ask-element`). No link and no name — that is the one question.
- The three usual questions — how it works, what it solves, how to integrate — need three kinds of evidence: the mechanism
  (documentation, the repository, the product itself in a test account the person confirmed); the value (its own pages, prices, dated
  reviews); the integration (API, SDK, MCP, webhooks, sign-in, limits, terms of use) and a live probe with a test key.
- The goal behind the question — to integrate, to build an analogue, to buy, to write about it, to decide — sets the depth.

## The facts the forces act on
- **Primary sources only.** A claim about a foreign service comes from its own pages, its repository or its terms, with the date it was
  read (CLAUDE.md §6). A site that forbids study gives only the person's screenshots, its inner pages only a test account the person
  confirmed (`npm run site-rules`).
- **A live probe needs this computer:** the SDK installed, a server and a client started, a call with a test key that lives in the
  environment of the hand that probes, never in the node's `.env.local`. Its scripts stay in that hand's git and run again later.
- **What a chat already does** — Anthropic's own pages, read 2026-11-08: Claude.ai's Research runs agentic searches that build on each
  other, with citations (anthropic.com/news/research, 2025-04-15); Integrations connect tools through remote MCP servers, and a research
  runs up to 45 minutes (anthropic.com/news/integrations, 2025-05-01). A one-off question with nothing to keep is answered well there,
  or here in a few lines.
- **What the node adds** — only this is said to the person: the tests run on this computer and their scripts stay in git; the research
  outlives any window — a letter and a clean window, never a compaction (core step 700); a page per research on the person's own
  domain, by their yes, and a dashboard behind sign-in; a clock for re-checks after a yes; every research in one data space and one graph
  (the data, vectors and graph elements), so researches are compared with one yardstick; other hands take a finished dossier over i2i;
  every hand works in its own window of up to a million tokens while the core keeps only the map.
- **The price:** a hand is about 150 MB of memory while awake and sleeps between researches; a study of a site through REA and its study
  browser holds up to about 1 GB while it runs; the strongest model's requests come from the person's subscription.
- **A research is not a study.** A study takes a product apart to build an analogue, in the element that will build it (`study-for-element`,
  its study pass); a research understands, compares and checks, and its dossier is what that element starts from.
- **Research that recurs learns** (force 6): a question asked again, a re-check, a comparison with the last one all need what was kept.

## How the work runs once the shape is chosen — one link at a time, each after the person's yes
1. **The question and the goal**, in the person's words, verbatim into `current-steps.md`.
2. **Whatever is new is born with its horizon and its starting vector** (`create-element`). The vector names its face — a public page
   per research, published by the person's yes, and an index of the published ones; its inner pages — a dashboard per research for the
   architect and the roles the person names: the question, the sources with their dates, the probes and what they showed, the verdict,
   the open questions, the next re-check; its loads — a static public layer, dashboards over its own tables, sleep between researches;
   and its door for the neighbours — what is known about a product, answered from its tables and the graph with no model.
3. **The record of a research:** the question, the sources with dates, the probes and their output, the verdict, what was not checked
   and why, the date of the next re-check.
4. **The probe** runs in the hand that holds the research, with a test key the person gives to that hand.
5. **The answer to the person** — the verdict first, then the evidence, then what was not checked; a page only after their yes.
6. **Integration** — the element that will integrate takes the dossier over i2i (`route-to-element`); which hand builds it, the core's
   map says.
7. **Community skills** for research — `skills_find` first; a skill goes into the hand that does the work, after the person's yes.

## Laws
- Every probe that spends money or calls an outside service, every publication and every rhythm of re-checks starts after the person's
  yes.
- No claim about a foreign service without its page and its date; no «nobody else does this»; what is on the way is said as on the way.
- The keys of an outside service live only in the hand that uses them; personal data never goes into git.
- An outside order for a research (a2a) is served by the hand's door, never by a Claude session on the owner's subscription
  (`provider-rules`); a2a is a plan until the Lagrix network lives.
