# The field of publishing

**Process: both.** The talk is the working process; whatever is born goes through `create-element` or the hand's own agent (build).
Node step 658; the owner, 2026-11-05: «обычно пользователи используют чистый Claude.ai для того чтобы везти свои социальные сети … но
можно достигнуть раза до больших результатов если таким образом использовать Fractera …». Step 673: this skill gives the facts of the
field; which shape the work takes — an answer, a route, a hand of its own, a boundary, the network — the method of `shape-the-need`
decides on the node's facts.

## Why it matters
Claude.ai alone writes a good post. Publishing is a loop — a channel, a voice, the numbers of each publication, the topic that rises —
and each publication can teach the next only where the loop is kept.

## The facts the forces act on
- **A platform** has its own account, API, rules on automation, spam and limits, and its own rhythm. Its rules are read on its own
  pages, never told from memory (`npm run site-rules`, skill `provider-rules`). Platforms, their accounts and their rhythms are routes,
  records and calendar entries of ONE publishing hand of one owner — never a hand each: a platform is not a boundary (force 5 of
  `shape-the-need`). A hand of its own only where force 5 differs — another owner's accounts, money or secrets, a face of its own.
- **A voice** — an author's style taken apart into as many components as possible — serves every channel that writes in it (force 4).
  It is built from the author's open texts that `site-rules` allows, or from what the person brings (CLAUDE.md §6: a site that forbids
  study gives only the person's screenshots); we take the techniques, never the texts.
- **The numbers** — every publication returns its statistics through the platform's official API; reading them and changing the next
  publication is what makes the loop grow (force 6). They belong to the one who publishes.
- **A rising topic** is seen through the official APIs and feeds of networks, channels, video and news portals, on a clock (force 6).
- **Media the node lacks** — image or video generation, montage, animation — is a capability many will reuse (force 4).
- **The person's own domain** can hold their long texts, besides the platforms; `npm run find` and the cards show which hand's area
  already covers a site.

## How the work runs once the shape is chosen — one link at a time, each after the person's yes
1. **The channels and the goal**, in the person's words: which networks, blogs, boards, forums; what the publications are for.
2. **Whatever is new is born with its horizon** (`create-element`, its area and what it may grow into); a new job of a field a hand
   holds is a route built by that hand's own agent.
3. **A portrait** — the style taken apart (`study-for-element`), then adapted to the person's product.
4. **A publication** — the one who holds a channel asks the one who holds the voices for a text, over the API the two agents agree
   (`route-to-element`, i2i). The person approves a draft, or gives a standing yes for one channel and one rhythm.
5. **The numbers** — read into the publisher's own tables and the deep memory (node step 654: its own space); an analysis at a rhythm
   the person chose says what works where — the topic, the format, the hour, the voice — and proposes the next changes.
6. **The topics** — the one who watches proposes a publication in trend to the channel that fits.
7. **Community skills** for posting and analytics — `skills_find` first.

## Laws
- Every publication, every posting rhythm and every round of watching starts after the person's yes; a resident process is named with
  its price in memory.
- Official APIs, feeds and the person's own accounts only.
- No tool of the node posts by itself today: the hand that holds a channel builds its publishing on that platform's API.
- The followers' personal data never goes into git; nothing is offered as working that the node cannot do today.
