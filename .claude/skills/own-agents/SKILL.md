# Own agents for the person's own effectiveness

**Process: both.** The talk and the order are the working process; an element is then born by the skill `create-element` (build).
Node step 658; the owner, 2026-11-05: «создать себе своих собственных агентов которые будут помогать тебе в достижения необходимых в тебе
качеств … Достаточно взять Telegram и сказать что тебе нужно один сам исследует лучшее решение в этой области продолжить тебе на
согласование создаст интерфейс для интерактивного взаимодействия или продолжить работать через Telegram».

## Why it matters
A person can have a coach, a dietitian, an economist, an investor and a keeper of their dates, each remembering everything about them —
on the deep memory, the object storage and the other stores of the node — and talk to them from the phone.

## The order
1. **The need** in the person's words: what quality, what result, how often they want to hear from the agent.
2. **The lightest shape** (`shape-the-need`): a date to remember (a tax declaration, a car's service, a document's expiry) is a reminder
   of the deep memory at a time (`npm run remember -- … --remind-at`), no element; a field with data and a rhythm (meals, training, a
   budget, a portfolio, a car's service book) is an element of its own with its own bot.
3. **The best way first** — `skills_find`, methods and cases from the internet with their sources; the proposal in a short living answer
   and the person's yes (`CLAUDE.md` §4: no plan in the first answer).
4. **The element** — `npm run find`, then `create-element` with the job, the method and the person's rhythm in the brief; its bot
   (`npm run telegram`); a page of its own only when the work needs a view (a chart of weight, a budget table, a portfolio), otherwise
   Telegram alone.
5. **What it keeps** — the facts in the deep memory, photos of receipts and documents as its own private files, its own tables, its own
   calendar for its reminders (node step 654: everything in its own space).

## Laws
- A dietitian, an economist or an investor agent is not a doctor or a licensed adviser: it says so, and decisions of health and money
  stay the person's.
- The person's data stays in their element and never goes into git; nothing is scheduled without the person's yes; nothing is offered
  as working that the node cannot do today.
