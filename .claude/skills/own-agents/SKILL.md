**Process: both.** The talk is the working process; whatever is born goes through `create-element` or the hand's own agent (build).

## Why it matters
A person can have help in every field of their own life, each remembering everything about them — on the deep memory, the object
storage and the other stores of the node — and talk to it from the phone.

## The facts the forces act on
- **The deep memory already keeps a fact with a time** and brings it back at that minute by itself (`npm run remember -- …
  --remind-at`, skill `use-deep-memory`) — a capability that exists (force 4).
- **A field with data and a rhythm** gathers measurements, history and numbers and gets better from them (force 6).
- **A view** — a chart, a table — is worth building only when the work has to be seen; a talk alone needs only a bot.
- **The data is the person's own**: photos of receipts and documents as private files, their own tables, their own calendar (node
  step 654: everything in its own space) — force 5 when it must not mix with anyone's.
- **Health and money**: an agent is not a doctor or a licensed adviser; decisions of health and money stay the person's.

## How the work runs once the shape is chosen
1. **The need** in the person's words: what quality, what result, how often they want to hear from the agent.
2. **The best way first** — `skills_find`, methods and cases from the internet with their sources; the proposal in a short living answer
   and the person's yes (CLAUDE.md §4: no plan in the first answer).
3. **Whatever is new is born** — `npm run find`, then `create-element` with the job, the method and the person's rhythm in the brief,
   and its bot (`npm run telegram`); a reminder goes through `use-deep-memory`.
4. **What it keeps** — the facts in the deep memory, the files, the tables and the calendar in its own space.

## Laws
- The agent says it is not a doctor or a licensed adviser.
- The person's data stays in their own space and never goes into git; nothing is scheduled without the person's yes; nothing is offered
  as working that the node cannot do today.
