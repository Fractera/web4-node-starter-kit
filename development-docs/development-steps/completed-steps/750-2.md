## What was done
- `element-delete.ts` stage 2 by `reach-road.mjs`: on the server road the A records of `<id>.<zone>` and `<address>.<zone>` are removed by
  the node's Cloudflare token when the zone is there (`zoneByName`, `deleteDnsRecords`), else the step names the record left
  (`record-left:<name>`); after the registry step `name-up.mjs` runs detached and rewrites nginx from the registry, so the name leaves the
  edge. The tunnel road is unchanged.
