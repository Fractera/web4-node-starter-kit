## What was done

- The core skill `.claude/skills/money-and-resources`: where the facts are (`npm run money`, `--balances`, the «Все подписки» page),
  what each provider tells (the 726-4 measurement), how to answer without a model and without a key — calls and tokens, never a sum from

- `redLines` in `lib/money-day.cjs`: today's red per provider from the core's ledger (refusing for money, budget reached, the pace not

  prints it and ends with ONE offer to check the balances.
