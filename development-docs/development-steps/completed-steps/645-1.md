# 645-1 — right drawers: full height, the content 25 px lower

**Commits:** `91c68209` (the result) after `a8a7a51a` (the first attempt, replaced) in `Fractera/web4-node-starter-kit` (main).

**What and how.** `components/ui/sheet.tsx`: a right drawer stays `inset-y-0 h-full`; a prop `barInset` (default for `side="right"`) adds
`pt-[25px]` AFTER the drawer's own classes — the account drawer's `p-0` keeps it — and moves the absolute ✕ to `top-[41px]`.
`components/menu/drawer/drawer-panel.client.tsx` (already under the header, `top-14`) passes `barInset={false}`. The fixed online bar of
the architect pages (30 px, z-60) no longer covers the title and the ✕.

**The owner's words.** First: «на всех правых выдвижных ящиках сверху сделала 40 пикселей». After the first attempt (the whole drawer
moved 40 px down): «Ты сломал выдвижной ящик, и теперь у нас дырка сверху … верни высоту выдвижного ящика … речь идёт о внутреннем
отступе … изменим его на 25».

**Proofs.**
1. `npm run eyes -- http://localhost:24680/ru/passport/how-it-works --click 'Личный кабинет' --measure …` in the owner's node (core
   `a36702d3`): `sheet-content` x 1120 y 0 h 900 (full height); title «Личный кабинет» y 41 — «виден»; ✕ y 41 — «виден»; five items y 98–218
   — «виден»; no console errors; the screenshot looked at. Negative control: the first attempt's class `top-10 bottom-0 right-0 h-auto` — 0
   files in the served chunks.
2. The served CSS: `.pt-\[25px\]{padding-top:25px}`, `.top-\[41px\]{top:41px}`; tailwind-merge on the account drawer gives
   `… p-0 gap-0 flex flex-col pt-[25px]`.

**Mistakes.** The first attempt was proven by «the class is in the build» and shown to the owner without being seen: the drawer moved as a
whole and left a hole on top, 40 px was too much. Then the owner's Safari showed the old drawer — a tab opened before the rebuild; the
node served the new one. Both led to step 647 (`npm run eyes`).
