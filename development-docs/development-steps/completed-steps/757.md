## What was done
- Reproduced in his Chrome on this Mac's node (core `e00c01b5`), his moves one by one: root → «Живой сайт» → «Подсветка», both banners
  closed («Больше не показывать», the owner's strip ×), r7hq2 «Нажми для обновления» → «Улучшить дизайн» → details «убрать лишнее
  отступ» → «Отправить» → the error screen. The console, verbatim: «Error: `Tooltip` must be used within `TooltipProvider`».
- The cause is mine: 742 (`6745d3c2`) replaced `title=` on the toolbar's three buttons (open in a new tab, reload, highlight) with
  `hint()` — a Tooltip with the button's name, rendered only while the terminal drawer is open (`compact`). The preview's only
  TooltipProvider wrapped the find form. «Отправить» opens the drawer → `compact` → three Tooltips outside a provider → Radix throws →
  the panel's error screen. Nothing reached root's agent: `onSend` only opens the drawer with the task in its paste window.
- The fix: one TooltipProvider around the whole preview — the find help and the toolbar's hints share it; the find form moved one
  indentation level (whitespace only).
- The class across the core: a scan of 657 `.tsx` for a `<Tooltip>` outside a `<TooltipProvider>` of its own file — 4 hits; the
  other 3 are safe: element-github's `Help` and menu-ai render only inside their parent's provider, prompt-input's `tooltip` prop has
  no caller.
