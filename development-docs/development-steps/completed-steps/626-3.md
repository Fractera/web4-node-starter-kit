## What was done
- `lib/data-store/context.mjs` (vectors): the OpenAI `embed()`, `openAiKey()` and the env model are gone; `embed(text)` asks the
  node's `embeddings` (`POST /api/embed`, `EMBEDDINGS_URL` by the installer's neighbour rule, `X-Node-Key` = the shared
  `SETTINGS_SECRET`); `embeddingsStatus()` — its `GET /api/embed-status`.
- `lib/data-store/app.mjs`: before the store opens, the model and size come from `embeddings`; a store recorded with another model
  (or of the layout before 626 — OpenAI) is **set aside whole**: `data/vectors.db` → `data/vectors.openai-<date>.db` (the journal
  checkpointed into it first, `-wal`/`-shm` renamed with it, nothing deleted), and a new store records its model in `store_meta`.
  A failed build (embeddings not answering) is forgotten — the next request builds again; meanwhile every door answers
  `503 embeddings_unavailable` with the reason (404 through the tunnel).
- `lib/data-store/vectors.mjs`: the dims of the `vec0` index and the size check come from the store's model; `/vectors/status`:
  `configured` — `embeddings` answers ready with the store's model; also `embeddings` and `setAside`.
- `.env.example`: `EMBEDDINGS_URL` (derived) instead of `EMBED_MODEL`, `EMBED_DIMS`, `APP_ENV_FILE`; `OPENAI_API_KEY` stays for the
  site's voice input and translations only. README: «Where the vectors come from», «Changing the model», «Environment».
