# 670-4 — `find` says «does it now / its area covers it / nobody», and why

## Why
Measured on the owner's node 2026-11-07: «сгенерировать контент для соцсетей» → storage 0.34, «пересказ ролика YouTube» → storage 0.38,
both above `FIND_MIN` 0.25 — storage matched on the words of its description («videos», «documents»), not on a capability. 626-4 already
measured that a threshold alone does not separate (the right element 0.118–0.572, absent jobs up to 0.284).

## What is built
- `lib/a2a/self-description.cjs` `indexPieces`: the area and each «may grow into» job become pieces of their own, each piece tagged with
  its kind (`skill`, `area`, `grows`, `description`, `role`); `findByMeaning` returns for every hit the kind and the text of its best
  piece. The index's freshness check counts the areas too (how the collection is refreshed is read first, then followed).
- `scripts/main-agent-cli.mjs find`: per hit — «does it now — skill <id>» · «its area covers it — a route to build there, by its own
  agent» · «weak — only words of its description match: <piece>»; «Nobody in this node does …» as before. The weak line is decided by a
  measured rule (kind + score), written in the code with the measurement.
- A measurement script over intents: the 20 intents and 6 absent jobs of 626-4 where they are in the repository, plus the owner's two
  examples; a before/after table into the summary.

## What counts as done
On today's node both of the owner's examples answer «nobody» (no element has that area) and storage is no longer a candidate; once an
area covers a job, `find` names that element as «its area covers it».

## How it is proven
1. **The measurement.** The table: the right element first as before (no loss on the 20 intents), absent jobs not printed as
   candidates. **Negative control:** with areas switched off the old false hit (storage 0.38) comes back.
2. **The live command on the owner's node** (after 670-7's update): «пересказ ролика YouTube» — no storage; «keep a voice message» —
   still memory.

## Rests on
670-3, 626-4 (pieces, the local model), 603-2 (search by meaning).

## Not in it
A model call in `find` (it stays free); the area of custom elements written by their agents (671).
