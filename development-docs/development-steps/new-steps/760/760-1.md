# 760-1 — «Запомнить сервер на 24 часа» on the launch page

**Built.** `components/hosting/server-launch.client.tsx`: a Switch right under the H2 «Запустить сервер», in the form and in the connected
ladder. `components/hosting/launch-remember.ts` (pure): read · save · forget `{host, port, until}` in this browser's localStorage, and the
switch's own flag. Words in `server-launch.i18n.ts` (ru, en): the label, its note («пароль не хранится — пока открыт доступ, он не
нужен»), «Пароль не нужен до {time}», the lead without «страница ничего не хранит» when the switch is on.
**Behaviour.** On + a successful connect → `{host, port, until: the visit's deadline}` kept. The next visit: step 1 filled, the password
optional with the note, «Проверить сервер» connects by the visit. Past `until`, off, «Закрыть доступ» → forgotten at once. The door answers
`need-password` → the IP stays, the password is asked. Off — the page is empty on every visit, as 738-4 (several servers).
**Done.** With the switch on, a reload brings 109.199.105.213 back in one click, without a password, while the access lasts.
**Proof.** (a) `scripts/launch-remember.test.mjs`: an entry past its deadline is not returned; the stored JSON holds exactly host, port,
until — negative control: a `password` handed to `save` is never written. (b) Chrome: switch on → reload → step 1 filled → one click → the
server's ladder; switch off → reload → an empty form.
**Rests on.** 744-3 (the visit cookie), `connect` with an empty password.
**Not in it.** Storing the password; several remembered servers (the owner: «если мой сервер один»); a connect without a click.
