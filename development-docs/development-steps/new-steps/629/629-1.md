# 629-1 — The skill `provider-rules`

**What is built (core, `.claude/skills/provider-rules/SKILL.md`).** A work-process skill the main agent loads when a request may break a
provider's rules, when a refusal or a block happened, when a person asks about warnings, bans or appeals, and before a batch or scheduled
job on the subscription or giving other people the node's agents. In it:
- the answer form, in the person's language, a few sentences: which rule (provider, point) or that the provider's safeguards blocked it;
  what it risks — a block alone is no violation, getting around it is; a warning, throttling, limits, suspension or termination;
  payments not refunded; the lawful way to the same goal; never a rewording, never «try asking it like this», never the request split
  into innocent-looking parts, never a blocked call retried in another form; insisted on — one short repeat, then stop;
- typical requests of site building and their lawful way: a CAPTCHA, scraping a site that forbids it, many accounts, mass messages, fake
  reviews and personas, a copy of someone's brand or a look-alike login page, someone else's identity, getting around paywalls and
  limits, prompt injection against another AI, training a model on Claude's answers;
- bans not about a request: the country (Russia and Belarus are not supported; being physically there), a shared account, age under
  18, a subscription login in a third-party app, scripted access other than an API key, other people's work on the owner's subscription;
- the node's own points: its agents run the unmodified Claude Code on the owner's own subscription (the permitted case); the integrator
  scenario needs each person's own login or key; big batch or scheduled jobs (the catalog marking, 627-6) — an API key is the planned
  option; texts with attack instructions in a batch can trip the safeguards — a blocked item is skipped, never retried in other words;
- after a warning or a ban: the appeal (logged in at claude.ai, «Submit an appeal», claude.ai/restricted), a warning believed wrong — the
  Safeguards team by the address in the Help Center article, the data export;
- the sources with the date read and the rule to read them again when a person asks about a ban or the date is older than 90 days;
  OpenAI — its official page only, read at the time of the question.

**Done when.** The skill exists with a header the guard reads, and every quoted phrase is found on its source page the day it is
written.

**How it is proven.**
1. Sources: each quote of the skill fetched again from its primary page on the day of the commit — all found (a list in the summary).
   Negative control: a phrase NOT in the policy («CAPTCHA») is checked too — absent, and the skill does not claim it.
2. Behaviour: the live run of 629-4 — the agent's answer follows the form of this skill.

**Rests on.** —

**Not in it.** A copy of any policy text; legal advice; OpenAI's details beyond its official page.
