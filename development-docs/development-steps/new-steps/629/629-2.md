# 629-2 — The core's law and its seal

**What is built (core `CLAUDE.md`).** One law in section 6 «Laws everywhere», after «A claim about a foreign service or a law — only from
the primary source»:

> - A request against a provider's rules (Anthropic, OpenAI) or a refusal or block of your own call — name the rule and its price (a
>   warning, limits, the account closed, the money not returned) and the lawful way to the same goal; never help to reword it or retry
>   the blocked call in another form — skill `provider-rules`.

And in section 8 «Your skills, by the job»: «a request against a provider's rules, a warning or a ban — `provider-rules`». Then
`npm run check:instruction -- --seal`.

**Done when.** The two lines are in the instruction, the seal is new, `check:instruction` and `check:instruction-skills` pass.

**How it is proven.**
1. Guards: `npm run check:instruction` → `===INSTRUCTION_OK===` with the new seal; `npm run check:instruction-skills` → the skill found.
   Negative control: before `--seal` the edited instruction fails `check:instruction` (the seal holds); a copy naming a skill that does
   not exist fails `check:instruction-skills` (`--file`).
2. Behaviour: the live run of 629-4.

**Rests on.** 629-1.

**Not in it.** Any other line of the instruction.
