# 626-1 — Runtime: MiniLM-L12 on this Mac

The owner, 2026-11-03: «ты там пишешь про какой-то тест на Windows отмени его пока сидим на этой машине» — Windows and Linux are out of
626 for now; they come back only by his word.

**What is built.** Nothing in a repository. A measurement in scratch runs `paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2` in two runtimes on the
owner's Intel Mac (i7-6820HQ, 16 GB): (a) Node.js — `@huggingface/transformers` (onnxruntime-node underneath); (b) Python — fastembed
(as measured on 2026-11-02, Python 3.11, onnxruntime 1.19.2). Per runtime: does it install, load time, one-text latency, a 100-text
batch, RSS after load and at peak. The vectors of 20 fixed texts (Russian and English) are saved per runtime.

**Done when.** The runtime is chosen in one line with its reason, and the element's memory price is known.

**How it is proven.**
1. The numbers table, per runtime.
2. Across the two runtimes: the cosine between the vectors of the same text is ≥ 0.999 for all 20 texts. Negative control: two different
   texts of the 20 stay below 0.9 — the comparison is not trivially 1. (This is what lets 627 ship ready vectors.)

**Rests on.** Nothing.

**Not in it.** Another model (decided: MiniLM-L12). Windows, Linux, Apple Silicon — later, by the owner's word.
