# 663-1 — the passport field `power` in every element (plan)

**Built.** `OWN-SERVICE-PROPS/OWN-SERVICE-PROPS.json` of the 15 repositories: `power: { default: "on"|"off", switch: "never"|"manual"|"auto",
wakeOnCall: boolean, reason? }` — never: auth, data, root, storage; manual: stripe (default off, its reason kept); auto + wakeOnCall:
embeddings, graph, memory, vectors, cache, config, design, blocks, the custom template (every custom element born from it). The guard of the
passport (`check:passport` or the element's existing passport guard) knows the field and refuses an unknown value. The core:
`lib/web4-items/element-power.cjs` reads `switch` (`switchOf(entry, passport)`; no field → `manual`, the old behaviour). The landing's power
section (662) is fed from the passport by `add-sections`-style data, the hand-written texts replaced.
**Done:** every element's passport says whether it may sleep, and the core reads it.
**Proven:** lab — the guard red on `switch: "sometimes"`, green on the 15 passports; the core's `switchOf` over the node's registry prints
the 13 classes as decided. CI three-os on all 15.
**Rests on:** 567 (`off`), 619 (only Stripe off by default).
**Not in it:** the rule itself (663-3).
