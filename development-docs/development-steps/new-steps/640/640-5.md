# 640-5 — The catalog hears the agent's terms, not only its sentence; five cards instead of three

**Why (found in 640-4, 2026-11-04 ~12:40 UTC).** The owner asked for writing «in the style of a blogger I like». The main agent (Opus)
called `skills_find` three times, with the terms «brand voice», «tone of voice», «voice profile», but the catalog answered without
`affaan-m/ecc@brand-voice`. The agent said «в проверенном каталоге Fractera такого навыка нет» and went to the open skills.sh. Asked by
the owner («я создавал тот самый каталог … эти навыки туда не попали?»), it found the card by hand and explained it wrongly: «на длинные
фразы он отвечает хуже». The owner: «check skill finding problem».

**Measured on the owner's live catalog (3240 cards, MiniLM-L12-v2) with the agent's exact calls** (probes `p642/rank.mjs`,
`p642/mean.mjs` in the scratchpad):
| the agent's query | brand-voice today | with its terms (mean of the sentence and the best term) |
|---|---|---|
| «Write content in the style, voice and format of a specific blogger the person likes» | #12, 0.619 | **#1, 0.714** |
| «Analyze sample texts of an author and extract a reusable writing style profile or voice guide» | #6, 0.604 | **#2, 0.691** |
| «I want to run a blog and publish content to social networks» (the control) | social skills #1–3 | social skills #1–3 |

Two causes in the code, neither is «a long sentence»:
1. `lib/agent-kit/skills/mcp.mjs` `call()` gives the catalog only `query`; `themes` and `terms` go to the graph memory only. The term
   «brand voice» alone puts the card at #2 with 0.809.
2. `findInCatalogFile` returns at most three cards (`n = 3`). The card was above the threshold (0.604, 0.619) and was cut by the count.
   An empty top three is not proof that the catalog has nothing, but the agent took it as one.
The max over the terms alone was also measured: the noise of a single word («social media» → remotion-multimedia) pushed the social
skills out of the top six. So the fusion is the mean of the two scores.

**What is built.**
- `catalog-index.mjs` `findInCatalogFile(root, { query, terms, n })`: the sentence and each term (themes and terms, at most 10) are
  embedded in one call. A card's score is the mean of its best piece for the sentence and its best piece for any term. Without terms
  the score is the sentence alone, as today. The threshold of 0.60 applies to that score. n defaults to 5.
- `mcp.mjs` `call()` passes `themes` and `terms` to the catalog too. The tool's description says «five catalog skills». It also asks
  for short English terms that name the method ("brand voice", "voice profile"), not the goal.
- `catalogFileText`: when nothing fits, the text says that only the closest were looked at. It tells the agent to try short terms again
  before it says «nothing», and before skills.sh.
- `scripts/skills.mjs market`, the door `POST /api/skills/catalog`, memory's `findSolution`: the same terms are passed where the caller
  has them.
- The own skills keep the sentence alone at 0.45, as measured in 640-2 (no re-measure in this substep).

**Done when.** The agent's three calls from the 640-4 transcript, replayed against the owner's catalog, put brand-voice in the answer.
The social control keeps its three social skills at the top.

**How it is proven.**
1. Replay (`p642/probe-640-5.mjs` through the real `findInCatalogFile`): a fixture of the three calls above and 10 more requests with
   their terms (the author's style, content, and unrelated ones from `own-20.json`). Before (HEAD) and after, printed side by side.
   Negative control: the unrelated requests get no writing or voice card, and the social control does not lose its cards.
2. Live: the owner asks once more on Opus, «хочу писать посты в стиле блогера, который мне нравится — какой навык?». The transcript
   shows brand-voice in the catalog part of `skills_find`'s answer on the first call, and the agent does not go to skills.sh.
