Two causes in the code, neither is «a long sentence»:
1. `lib/agent-kit/skills/mcp.mjs` `call()` gives the catalog only `query`; `themes` and `terms` go to the graph memory only. The term
   «brand voice» alone puts the card at #2 with 0.809.
2. `findInCatalogFile` returns at most three cards (`n = 3`). The card was above the threshold (0.604, 0.619) and was cut by the count.
   An empty top three is not proof that the catalog has nothing, but the agent took it as one.
The max over the terms alone was also measured: the noise of a single word («social media» → remotion-multimedia) pushed the social
skills out of the top six. So the fusion is the mean of the two scores.

**What is built.**
- `catalog-index.mjs` `findInCatalogFile(root, { query, terms, rephrasings, n = 7 })`, in passes until seven cards:
  1. the sentence with the agent's terms: a card's score is the mean of its best piece for the sentence and its best piece for any
     term (no terms — the sentence alone), at 0.60;
  2. each of the agent's 2–3 rephrasings the same way, adding cards not yet taken;
  3. the other way: the words. The terms as whole phrases in a card's name (2) and its summary and tasks (1). A card needs 2. One
     common word in a summary was measured as noise: «reminder» brought seven unrelated cards. Skip-cards are never taken.
  The answer names each card's pass (`by`) and the passes (`passes`). The own skills keep the sentence alone at 0.45 (640-2).
- `catalogFileText`: seven is a first look, not the choice. Fewer than seven with fewer than two rephrasings: «call skills_find again
  with 2–3 rephrasings from the side of the method and short terms; do not say "nothing in the catalog" before that». Fewer after
  them: the checked catalog has no more, and the agent looks in skills.sh itself, named not checked by Fractera. The sealed line of
  CLAUDE.md section 1 changed for it, shown word for word with a Russian reading; the owner, 2026-11-04 ~13:30: «да, запечатай» —
  seal `72eed396d4b8`.
- `mcp.mjs`: `skills_find` passes themes, terms and the new `rephrasings` to the catalog; its description says «up to seven».
- `scripts/skills.mjs market` (`--terms`, `--again "<s1> | <s2>"`, `--limit` 7), the door `POST /api/skills/catalog` (`terms`,
  `rephrasings`, n defaults to 7).

**Done when.** The agent's calls from the 640-4 transcript, replayed against the owner's catalog, put brand-voice in the answer. The
social control keeps its social skills at the top. An unrelated request gets no noise.
