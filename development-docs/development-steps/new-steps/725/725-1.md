# 725-1 — the parts map: every reusable part has a card and a place in one map

**What is built.** A card `part.json` beside each reusable composite of the core (`components/form/voice-field`, `voice-textarea`,
`voice-control`; `components/dialog/app-dialog`; `components/workspace/workspace-shell`, `menu-state-dot`; the vendored
`components/ai-elements` as one family) and one card per primitive kind of `components/ui` (the «one owner per kind» of `use-primitives`):
`{ id, kind: tool|section|composite|primitive, path, what, how, needs, replaces: [import patterns it supersedes], words: {en, ru} }`.
`npm run build:parts-map` writes `PARTS.json` — tools (from `TOOLS.json`), sections (from `SECTIONS.json`), composites, primitives — with
`usedBy` measured from the imports. `check:parts-map` in prebuild: a stale map; a card pointing to a missing file; a component under
`components/` imported from two or more other folders without a card → red, naming it.

**Done when.** The map holds all four kinds and the guard is in prebuild.

**Proven by.** Plane 1 — build and check green; negative control: a scratch component imported from two folders without a card → the
guard red with its path; removed → green. Plane 2 — `PARTS.json` lists `VoiceTextarea` with `usedBy` including
`components/element-docs/reverse-engineering.client.tsx` (measured today) and the chat tool with its users.

**Rests on.** The tools and sections maps. **Not in it.** Search (725-2), enforcement of `replaces` (725-3).
