## The sample and where each page stands
The sample: `fractera-stripe-starter` `widget-static-stripe-landing` (step 573-7) — a bare page drawn by its own widget from its data
blocks: a section is `block-section-head` and the blocks up to the next, its view chosen by its `id`, an unknown `id` drawn by the
common view; the order: hero · «How a … goes» (four stations) · «What changes for you» (table) · the element's own sections · «Connect
your element» (a real door call) · «Under the hood» (table) · «Open source». Measured on 2026-11-05:

## What is built
- In memory, graph and vectors: `components/blocks/block-access-map.tsx` and its card from Blocks v1.4.19 (the same files `npm run blocks
  -- add` installs), drawn by the landing widget for the section `spaces`.
- Graph and vectors: the landing widget copied from the sample with its own icons and section views; `_data/meta.json` `bare: true`;
  `_data/en.json` and `ru.json` in the sample's order; `markdown.ts` and the README of the widget.
- The words: en first, ru the same; every claim checked against the element's README and code (only what is built); `humanizer` over
  every text, the sample's voice.
- The core: «Как это работает» §5 — part 5.5 «Память у каждого своя» (words); the kit's README «Deep memory» — a paragraph and a Mermaid
  scheme of the spaces.
