**What is built (core).**
- `scripts/catalog/census.mjs`: the skills.sh leaderboard top-down (`GET /api/v1/skills?view=all-time&page=N&per_page=500`, a Vercel
  OIDC token — the owner's free Vercel account, `vercel link` + `vercel env pull` in a folder outside the node) until **5,000 alive,
  unique, risk-free skills** (run 1: ~17 % of rows are gone, ~3 % duplicates → ~6,000–6,500 rows). No cut by stars or installs: the order
  is the leaderboard's, nothing is filtered by popularity. Without a token: a sweep of `npx skills find` over topics (4–11 a query).
- Each card also carries the partners' audits (`GET /api/v1/skills/audit/<source>/<skill>` answers without a token — measured
  2026-11-02: Gen Agent Trust Hub «pass» for `find-skills`) and `quality` 1–5 read from the skill's own files (not its popularity).
- Categories, two levels at most: built ONCE after the collection from all cards' tags (one consistent tree «section → subsection»),
  then every card gets its section and subsection; thin sections are filled by topic searches. Built while marking in parallel, the same
  section would be invented under several names.
- Then fetch → mark → build → pack → release; `lib/agent-kit/skills/catalog/release.json` points to the new package.
- Order changed by the owner's word: the collection runs before 627-2 … 627-4 (it needs no vectors); the graph is built from it after 626.

**Done when.** The release holds every skill of the census, minus duplicates and refusals — each of them counted.

**How it is proven.**
1. The manifest's count equals census − duplicates − refusals (the three numbers in the summary).
2. 20 random cards read against their `SKILL.md`.
   Negative control: 5 known spam copies are absent.

**Rests on.** 627-4, and the owner's yes on the count, the time and the cost measured in 627-1 — asked in the choice window with those numbers.

**Not in it.** Skills from sources that are not GitHub.
