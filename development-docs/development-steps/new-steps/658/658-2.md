# 658-2 — own agents for one's own effectiveness: the main agent's skill, the instruction's lines (core)

**Repository:** `Fractera/web4-node-starter-kit` `main`.

## What is built

- **Skill `.claude/skills/own-agents/SKILL.md`** (both natures; the form of `integrator`): why it matters — a person can have a coach, a
  dietitian, an economist, an investor and a keeper of their life's dates, each remembering everything about them, and talk to them from
  Telegram. The order:
  1. the need in the person's words: what quality, what result, how often they want to hear from the agent;
  2. **the lightest shape** (`shape-the-need`): a date to remember (a tax declaration, a car's service, a document's expiry) — a reminder
     of the deep memory at a time (`npm run remember -- … --remind-at`), no element; a field with data and a rhythm (meals, training, a
     budget, a portfolio, a car's service book) — an element of its own with its own bot;
  3. **the best way first** — `skills_find`, methods and cases on the internet, with their sources; the proposal in a short living
     answer and the person's yes (`CLAUDE.md` §4 «A new product or a large capability is asked for» — no plan in the first answer);
  4. **the element** — `npm run find` → `create-element` with the job, the method and the person's rhythm in the brief; its bot
     (`npm run telegram`); a page of its own only when the work needs a view (a chart of weight, a budget table, a portfolio) — otherwise
     Telegram alone;
  5. **what it keeps** — the facts in the deep memory, photos of receipts and documents as private files of its own (654-2), its tables
     (654-1), its calendar for its own reminders.
  **Laws:** a dietitian, an economist or an investor agent is not a doctor or a licensed adviser — it says so, and decisions of health
  and money stay the person's; the person's data stays in their element and never goes into git; nothing is scheduled without the
  person's yes; nothing offered as working that the node cannot do today.
- **`shape-the-need`:** a pointer — agents for the person themselves are skill `own-agents` (beside «becoming a trainer or a dietitian»,
  which is about earning).
- **`CLAUDE.md`** (the owner's word — the yes to this plan): §1 «Why a node is built» gets «**Own agents:** a coach, a dietitian, an
  economist, an investor, a keeper of life's dates — for the person themselves.»; §4 one scenario line «**Own agents:** … — skill
  `own-agents`.»; §8 a pointer by the job; resealed.

## Done when

The main agent, given the own agents card's task, answers with the lightest shape for the person's need and asks one question.

## How it is proven (named in advance)

1. **Guards, with negative controls:** `check-instruction` with the new seal (before the reseal → red); `check-instruction-skills` (the
   skill folder moved away → `✗ own-agents — named by CLAUDE.md`); `check-skill-headers`; the broken-characters scan; CI three-os green.
2. **A live answer on the owner's node** after the delivery (one session of the main agent, named before it runs): «Хочу себе агента,
   который напомнит о налоговой декларации» → a reminder of the deep memory, no new element; «Хочу агента-диетолога, который ведёт мой
   рацион» → an element with its own bot after the best methods are found, one question back; **negative control:** «Хочу агентов для
   жены и сына» → `integrator`, not `own-agents`.
