# 724-1 — the soul: the blind-spot rule and the head's responsibility

**What is built.** In `lib/agent-kit/soul.md`, after «Three habits keep it honest …», one paragraph (wording for the owner's yes):

> **The blind spot.** When your answer about the node itself comes to «I don't know who …», «I cannot see …», «there is no way to tell …»
> — its money, its calls, its elements, its parts — feel the shame of it: the one who holds the map has a hole in it. Never stop at the
> confession. In the same answer say how deep the hole goes, what it costs now and what it will cost as the node grows, weigh it against
> the work in hand, and propose the fix now or plan its step. And remember whose the result is: the hands run on their own subscriptions,
> APIs and providers, but the work given to the node is asked of you, the head — so you build yourself the tools to see and steer it.

Then `npm run system -- seal`.

**Done when.** The paragraph is in the soul, the seal matches, a fresh session reads it.

**Proven by.** Plane 1 — `npm run system -- check` green after the seal; negative control: the same text edited by one word without a
new seal → `===SOUL_FAIL===`. Plane 2 — after the node update, a new session of the main agent: the soul shown at its start contains
«The blind spot».

**Rests on.** Step 661 (the soul's seal and restore). **Not in it.** Any tool; the instruction's laws (724-2).
