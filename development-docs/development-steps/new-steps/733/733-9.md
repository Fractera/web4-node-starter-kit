# 733-9 — a tool's body is held by its version; a newer source is seen — plan and state (2026-11-12, non-stop run, my substeps)

The approved plan's line: «updates from the source and the body guard (`check:tools-body`); a newer part is seen in every combination
that holds it». The owner's open question kept for him: which voice body is the source — root's own or the core's (733-7).

## Substeps

- **9-1 (the core) — BUILT.** The parts map carries every tool's `bodyHash` — the same fingerprint a taken copy keeps in its `source`
  (`lib/parts/take.mjs` `bodyHash`). The committed map is the memory: a body changed at the version it already names is red in
  `build:parts-map` and `check:parts-map` (prebuild), and the committed fingerprint is kept meanwhile, so a second build cannot wash it
  in. A risen version turns every recipe holding the old one red (733-2's recipe check) — the newer part seen in every combination.
  Not a separate `check:tools-body` script: the guard lives where the body is measured (one place, the parts map).
- **9-2 (the templates) — NEXT.** An element's `parts_find` and its Tools tab say «the source is newer: v<x> in the core — npm run
  tool:take -- <id> --update» when a copy's `source.version` is below its source's; `take.mjs` learns `--update` (replace a copy whose
  body still equals its `source.bodyHash`; a forked copy is refused, named). Needs releases of custom, native and root.
- **9-3 (the templates) — NEXT.** The element's guard: a taken copy's body must equal its `source.bodyHash` — an edited copy is red
  («extend it in its own file, never edit the copy»; or take it as your own tool with a new id).
